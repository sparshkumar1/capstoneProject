# Paper 2 - limitations to state in the paper
Author-constructed benchmark (64 answers to 8 questions written by the study team), 8 question clusters, answers not independent within a question; three raters whose provenance, consent and ethics records are incomplete (do not write "independent experts", "committee" or "approved"); consensus gold with adjudicated items; exploratory analysis with no confirmatory data (the confirmatory round is blocked pending an institutional/ethics determination); the R component is a partially fine-tuned derivative with incomplete training-data provenance so overlap with the benchmark cannot be excluded; the length structure of the answer categories confounds length-only baselines; category-level diagnostics rest on 2-8 cases per category; metamorphic and adversarial tests use author-set ceilings and tiny N; the composite is safety-hardened and is not shown better than simpler signals; intervals are wide.
