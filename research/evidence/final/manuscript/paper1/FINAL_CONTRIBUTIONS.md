# Paper 1 - permitted contribution statements (scoped; exact wording follows the claim matrix)
1. A claim-scoped fault-injection campaign over the enumerated request-path dependencies of an LLM-assisted assessment orchestrator, with a no-fault control and oracle-perturbation controls per scenario, that found two defects on the first build; the defects were repaired on a second build and both builds' results are reported side by side.
2. A containment campaign for the C code sandbox in which nine attack programs run against the shipped configuration and against a permissive control that shows each oracle can fail; with the layer-by-layer facts that qualify the result (static filter blocks only the literal ptrace call; socket() succeeds but connect() fails without a network; status strings do not discriminate contained from breached for four attacks).
3. An invariance experiment for score/difficulty/best-answer observables against LLM-authored text with a mutation control and a static guard, reported with its actual valid-sample count against the registered minimum, and with the follow-up-question channel documented as a dependency.
4. A dated record of application defects found by the campaigns, their repair and re-test, and the limitations that remain.
Not contributions: a security guarantee, fault tolerance, formal authority separation, novelty of the phenomena, any claim about users.
