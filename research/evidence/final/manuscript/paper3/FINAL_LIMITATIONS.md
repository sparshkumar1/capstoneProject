# Paper 3 - limitations to state in the paper
Simulation only, no human learners. Reward contains oracle-alignment components and the persona target is an authored rule (coupling not re-audited). 39 of 40 grid personas unseen in training; checkpoints trained against one default candidate with unseeded noise (not regenerable). Guardrails were in the training loop. Five training seeds are a hypothetical population (df = 4 in the seed-level analysis). The +/-0.12 margin is a design choice with no literature justification found. MAE ignores path shape (of 25 path-differing sessions only 5 differ in MAE). No Elo/IRT/CAT or knowledge-tracing baseline. The guardrail is a rule-based constraint layer, not a formal shield. The deployed demo policy differs from the X3-A training state definition. Registration tags are local, unpushed and not externally timestamped. Equivalence is not "no effect": O7-A and two leave-one-out intervals exclude zero on the PPO-favourable side.
