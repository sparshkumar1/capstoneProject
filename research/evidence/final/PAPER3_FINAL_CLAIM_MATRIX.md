# Paper 3 — final claim matrix (2026-09-20)

**Frame (locked 2026-09-21; wording only, no result changed).** Controlled policy decomposition / equivalence evaluation, **in simulation only** ("A Controlled Equivalence Study of Learned and Constant Difficulty Policies in Simulated Technical Interviews"): under an identical application-level rule-based guardrail layer, what a learned PPO difficulty controller adds beyond a simpler state-blind Constant-Same policy in simulated technical-interview trajectories. Not: PPO superiority, state-of-the-art adaptive interviewing, evidence that RL improves interviewing, or a new application, RL method, simulator or benchmark. **Prior art:** adaptive mock-interview tutoring with simulation, an IRT-based learner model, a finite-horizon MDP and a heuristic/DQN/PPO/PETS/MBPO comparison is established by Kadam et al. 2026 (only its Table A elements were verified; the body was not read); the contribution is the controlled decomposition and preregistered equivalence evaluation (registration tags are local and unpushed). **Terminology:** always "application-level rule-based guardrail"; never a formal, safe-RL or verified shield and never a safety guarantee. Registered artifacts, registry ids and the registered interpretation string still use the word "shield" for this layer; those strings are quoted verbatim only where cited and are read as "application-level rule-based guardrail". Statistical unit = the persona (training seed = second random factor); sessions and turns are descriptive. X3-B was not run and is not triggered. Registry ids in brackets; hashes are sha256 prefixes of the stored artifacts.

## 3. Explicitly unsupported (do not write)
PPO improves or is superior on tracking; PPO lowers volatility; guardrails eliminate boundary violations or establish learned-policy benefit; "no effect" from equivalence; real-interview, human-learning or deployment claims; multimodal/speech robustness; "state-of-the-art" or "outperforms Elo/IRT/CAT" (none was run); that PPO is generally inferior outside this environment; use of O7 to relabel the registered result; "shield" for the rule-based layer.

## 4. Structural limitations that travel with every Paper 3 claim
Simulation only; the persona target is an authored rule and the reward contains oracle-alignment components (coupling not re-audited); 39 of 40 grid personas unseen in training; five training seeds are a hypothetical population; MAE ignores path shape; no Elo/IRT/CAT baseline; ±0.12 margin unjustified by literature; X3-B (retraining/broader personas) not run; the frozen-evidence tags are local and unpushed. Action mix: the training-time Same share of the stochastic policy (last logging window) was 0.5069–0.5345 across the five training seeds (24,576 timesteps each; `research/analysis/phase1/x3_0/x3_0a_training_action_shares.csv`; `X3_0A_REPORT.md` item 7); the evaluation-time Same share of the X3-A trajectories is not stored in any summary and is not reported.
