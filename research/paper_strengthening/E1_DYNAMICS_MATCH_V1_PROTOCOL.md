## 1. Phase 1 source-audit findings (verified against current source, this session)

**A. Difficulty step size — confirmed real, as documented.** Training (`rl/env/interview_env.py:401-405`, unchanged): `Easier: difficulty = max(0.1, difficulty-0.1)`; `Harder: difficulty = min(1.0, difficulty+0.1)`. Evaluation (`x3a_lib.py`/`e1_simulate_adapter.py`, unchanged): `cur = clip(cur + {-1,0,+1}, 1.0, 5.0)`, candidate called with `cur/5.0`. Training step (±0.1 on a [0.1,1.0]-normalized scale) vs. evaluation step (±0.2 once normalized) — **evaluation moves normalized difficulty 2× as fast per action.**

**B. Episode horizon — confirmed real, as documented.** Training: `InterviewEnv.__init__`'s default `max_steps=15` (`interview_env.py:63`), unchanged by every training path E1 uses. Evaluation: `max_steps=10` (both `x3a_lib.simulate` and `e1_simulate_adapter.simulate_diverse`'s default).

**C. Initial difficulty — confirmed real, as documented.** Training (`interview_env.py:306`): `self.difficulty = float(self.np_random.uniform(0.3, 0.7))` when `initial_difficulty` is not passed (never passed anywhere in E1's training path). Evaluation: always starts at fixed `cur=3.0` → normalized `0.6`.

**D. A fourth, closely-related mismatch found and reported before expanding the design (Phase 1.5):** the difficulty **floor** also differs — training clips to `[0.1, 1.0]`; evaluation's floor is effectively `0.2` (`1.0/5.0`, since `cur` never goes below `1.0`). This is not independent of Factor A — it is a direct consequence of deriving the "matched" step size from evaluation's own `±1`-on-`[1,5]` mechanics. **Decision:** folded into Factor A's "matched" level (`step_size=0.2, floor=0.2`) rather than added as a fourth orthogonal factor, to avoid a 16-arm design; ceiling is `1.0` in both training and evaluation already, so no ceiling change is needed. Stated explicitly here per the instruction to report, not silently absorb, any further mismatch found.

**No other undocumented dynamics mismatch was found** in `interview_env.py`'s `step()`/`reset()`, `x3a_lib.py`'s `simulate()`, or `e1_simulate_adapter.py`'s `simulate_diverse()` (both re-read in full this session; both confirmed unmodified since E1's own registration, hashes re-verified: `x3a_lib.py` `3badbb39...`, `interview_env.py` `117af6c8...`).

**E. PPO learning-rate schedule (Phase 1.6) — confirmed constant, not decaying.** `e1_config.json`'s `learning_rate: 3e-4` and `clip_range: 0.2` are plain floats; SB3's `get_schedule_fn`/`FloatSchedule` wraps a plain float in a constant function that ignores `progress_remaining` entirely (verified by reading the installed SB3 0.30.0-line source directly). **Consequence:** in principle, a checkpoint saved mid-way through a longer `total_timesteps` run should be produced by the same update sequence as an independently-budgeted run at that step count, since nothing in PPO's per-update logic depends on the *declared* `total_timesteps` value except the outer stopping condition.

**This was checked empirically, not just theoretically** (one spot-check, seed 42, 16,384 steps): a **fresh** independent 16,384-step B2 training run was compared against the existing checkpoint-trace's 16,384-step snapshot (from the 98,304-step run). **Result: the two checkpoints are *not* bit-identical in weights** (max absolute parameter difference ≈0.070 — a real, non-trivial divergence, most likely from the extra `CheckpointCallback`/`WandbCallback` present only in the trace run consuming some shared RNG/logging path differently, not investigated further here) **but produce bit-identical pooled dev-eval MAE** (`0.26666...` for both, to full float precision). **Interpretation, stated precisely:** an intermediate checkpoint from a longer scheduled run is a good, empirically-supported proxy for "what a model independently trained to that budget would do" *on this specific evaluation metric*, for at least this one tested (seed, step) combination — but it is **not proven identical at the weight level**, and this iteration explicitly distinguishes the two (§4/§5 below) rather than treating them as interchangeable without disclosure, per the task's instruction.

**F. Reconciling V2's gate-activation denominators (Phase 1.7).** `E1_IMPROVE_V2_REPORT.md` §5 stated Condition A's `G6-nervous` gate "fires on 45/3000 steps... 2,373 total post-shield-overridden steps" — **the `3000` denominator was an error**, an editing artifact; recomputed directly from the raw output: **45 gate-fired steps out of 6,000 possible steps (600 episodes × 10 steps) = 0.75%**, and **2,373/6,000 = 39.6%** is the pooled count of *all* guardrail overrides across the whole episode set (from every guardrail rule, not specifically caused by this gate) — the two figures use the same correct denominator (6,000) but count structurally different things and should never have been juxtaposed as if comparable. Corrected here before any of these numbers are reused.

## 2. Factorial design (Phase 2)

All three documented mismatches (A/B/C) are confirmed real (§1); per the task's instruction, a full **2×2×2 factorial** on B2 is used.

| Factor | Original | Matched |
|---|---|---|
| A — difficulty step size (+ floor, per §1.D) | `step=0.1, floor=0.1, ceiling=1.0` (frozen `interview_env.py`) | `step=0.2, floor=0.2, ceiling=1.0` (evaluation-equivalent) |
| B — episode horizon | `max_steps=15` (frozen default) | `max_steps=10` (evaluation's) |
| C — initial difficulty | `Uniform(0.3,0.7)` (frozen default, unset `initial_difficulty`) | fixed `0.6` (evaluation's `cur=3.0/5.0`) |

**8 arms** (`ALL_8_ARMS`, labeled by first letter of each factor level, e.g. `OOO`=all-original, `MMM`=all-matched, `MOM`, etc. — `e1dynmatch_env_variant.py::arm_label_for`). **Everything else held constant**: same B2 candidate-generation grid/config (`e1_config.json`, `e1_diverse_persona_env.py`, unmodified), same reward function, observation/action definitions, guardrails, evaluator, target formula, and evaluation simulator (`x3a_lib.shield`, `e1_simulate_adapter.simulate_diverse`, unmodified); same PPO hyperparameters across all 8 arms (`e1_config.json`'s `ppo_hyperparameters`, unchanged); same 5 training seeds `[42,123,456,789,999]`, paired across arms (seed 42 in every arm uses the same seed value, enabling paired within-seed contrasts); evaluation on `EVAL_SEEDS_DEV` only, on the existing 24-cell `(skill,k)` grid.

**Implementation (presented for review, already written and unit-tested — no training run yet):** `research/paper_strengthening/scripts/e1dynmatch_env_variant.py`. Two of the three factors need **no new environment code at all** — `max_steps` (Factor B) and `initial_difficulty` (Factor C) are already-existing, unmodified `InterviewEnv` constructor parameters; only Factor A needs a new, minimal subclass (`DynamicsVariantEnv`) that lets the frozen `step()` run in full (so reward, computed entirely from the *pre*-step observation, is untouched — verified by `test_reward_at_first_step_is_unaffected_by_difficulty_patch`) and then overwrites only the resulting `self.difficulty`/`obs[5]` using the configured step/floor/ceiling instead of the frozen `0.1/0.1/1.0` constants. 10/10 new unit tests pass (`research/paper_strengthening/scripts/tests/test_e1dynmatch_env_variant.py`), including: observation/action space dimensions preserved (`Box(0,1,shape=(6,))`, `Discrete(3)`); the `original` level reproduces the frozen step/floor exactly; the `matched` level uses exactly `0.2`/`0.2`; no observation ever leaves `[0,1]`; Factor B/C kwargs propagate correctly; reward is bit-identical at step 0 (before any divergence is possible) across all three raw actions; a divergent Easier/Harder action legitimately produces different post-step difficulty once step sizes differ (confirming the intervention has a real, intended effect); the `OOO` arm's parameters exactly match the frozen environment's own constants (a direct equivalence check, satisfying Phase 4.3's "verify the all-original control reproduces the registered training behavior" — verified now, at the unit level, before any full training run).

## 3. Predeclared comparisons and criteria (fixed now, before any new training)

- **Primary total training budget: 24,576 steps** for all 8 arms (matching E1's own registered primary budget, for direct comparability).
- **Intermediate checkpoint reporting:** each arm/seed's single 24,576-step training run additionally saves checkpoints at 8,192 and 16,384 steps (`CheckpointCallback`, no extra training runs) — reported as **intermediate snapshots of a 24,576-step schedule**, explicitly distinguished (§1.E) from an independently-budgeted model, never conflated with one in the results write-up.
- **Primary comparison: `MMM` vs. `OOO` at 24,576 steps**, paired by training seed. This is the single decisive contrast.
- **Secondary, exploratory analyses (descriptive only, no significance claims):** one-factor main effects (mean MAE difference from flipping each factor alone, averaged over the 4 arms sharing the other two factors' levels) and two-/three-way interaction contrasts (standard 2×2×2 factorial contrasts), all reported as exploratory decomposition, not hypothesis tests.
- **Extended-budget (98,304-step) trigger, fixed now:** the `OOO`-vs-`MMM` comparison is re-run **from scratch** at 98,304 steps (both arms, all 5 seeds, independently trained — never obtained by continuing the 24,576-step run) **if and only if**, at the primary 24,576-step budget, `MMM`'s pooled dev MAE is **at least 0.02 lower** than `OOO`'s **and** `MMM` shows **no per-cell regression ≥0.05** relative to `OOO`. If this does not hold, the extended-budget comparison is not run, and this is reported as the reason no long-training-degradation test was performed.
- **Practical-improvement criterion (reused, not redefined):** the same rule used throughout E1/E1-Improve — a configuration is a genuine improvement over the `OOO` control only if it has strictly lower pooled dev MAE **and zero per-cell regressions ≥0.05**.
