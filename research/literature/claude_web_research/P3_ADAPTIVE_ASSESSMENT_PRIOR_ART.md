## Does Paper 3 remain distinct?
Answers describe the recommended framing (decomposition and equivalence), not "PPO for adaptive interview difficulty".
- **CAT:** distinct in object (session-level difficulty control for practice versus measurement of ability). Not distinct in machinery: IRT, simulated examinees, DQN/bandit selection and candidate-set filtering are established (A1, A2, A4).
- **IRT:** distinct only in that IRT is not the method under study. A reviewer will ask why a difficulty-tracking IRT/Elo baseline was not run (Pelanek 2016; A1; Kadam's simulator uses IRT).
- **RL-based item selection:** distinct in the question asked (is the learned component doing work under identical constraints?), not in the use of RL.
- **Adaptive tutoring:** distinct in the control-comparison design; the observation that simple or uniform policies rival learned ones is now reported in three places (A6, A7, A8).
- **Adaptive interview tutoring:** overlap is high at the application level (Kadam); distinct only at the contribution level.
- **Heuristic adaptive difficulty:** the project compares against one heuristic; the literature suggests Elo/IRT-style trackers as the standard heuristic (Pelanek). Not run.

## Change to the Paper 3 novelty assessment
- Direction unchanged: PPO-for-adaptive-difficulty, the interview setting and the simulator are not novelty claims; the decomposition/equivalence result remains the residual contribution.
- Strengthened caution: A6 and A7 (with A8) show "a learned policy is not clearly better than a simple one" is a recurring empirical pattern, so the observation itself is not novel; the preregistered, guardrail-matched, persona-level equivalence test is what remains. Confidence for P3-C1 is lowered from medium-high to medium.
- Guardrail concept: A1's candidate-window filter is an additional prior for a constraint applied around a learned selector; keep "application-level rule, not a formal shield".
- No newly found paper occupies the exact design (identical guardrails on a learned and a state-blind constant policy, preregistered equivalence margin), within the searched scope and with Kadam's methods unread.
