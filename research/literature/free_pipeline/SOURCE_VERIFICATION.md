# Source verification log (core papers)

For every core source: bibliographic identity, why selected, which claims it bears on, evidence basis (abstract vs full-text passages), whether each quote was locally checked, OA/PDF status and hash, and unresolved uncertainties. `quote_check=verified` means the quote is a verbatim (whitespace/punctuation-normalised) substring of the text supplied to the model. UNVERIFIED items are listed for transparency and are NOT used in the matrices.
