# X3-A Protocol v1 - Controlled decomposition on frozen PPO checkpoints (2026-09-19)

**Status: FINAL for tagging (`prereg/X3-A/v1`).** Evaluation-only, simulation-only. No training, no reward/state/action/guardrail/simulator change, no hyperparameter search. Normative machine-readable parameters: `research/confirmatory/X3-A/x3a_config.json` (hashed in `protocol_manifest.json`). Authority: the user's sprint instruction of 2026-09-19 (locked decisions in `research/audit/PHASE0_DECISION_LOCK.md`). Independent methodology review (ChatGPT) of this protocol has **not** been performed; it is tagged under the sprint authorisation and may be amended (new tag) only before confirmatory data exist.

## 1. Question and hypotheses
Q: in the simulator, how much of the target-tracking performance of "PPO + shield" is attributable to the shield, to rule-based policies (oracle rules, heuristic, proportional controller) and to the learned policy, once a trivial state-blind policy receives the same shield?
- **H1 (primary, equivalence/superiority framework).** Let Delta = persona-level tracking-MAE difference **PPO+G minus Constant-Same+G** (negative = PPO better). Classification (frozen): *Equivalent* if the 95 % CI is entirely inside (-0.12, +0.12); *PPO-superior* if the CI upper bound < -0.20; *PPO-adverse* if the CI lower bound > +0.12; otherwise *Inconclusive*. "Inconclusive" is never reported as "no effect". delta = 0.12 MAE, m = 0.20 MAE.
- Secondary and exploratory analyses are descriptive (section 5); no error control across them.

## 2. Fixed context (frozen, not changed by X3-A)
Five frozen checkpoints `research/experiments/paper3/checkpoints/seed_{42,123,456,789,999}/{ppo_final.zip,vecnormalize.pkl}` (hashes in `protocol_manifest.json`); evaluation `deterministic=True`; session = 10 turns, integer steps +-1, start difficulty 3.0; guardrail module `rl/guardrails.py` (validated local copy). Disclosed properties of the frozen training (X3-0b, registry `P3-C017..C019`): a single default candidate (skill 0.6, `normal`), unseeded candidate noise, and the shield active inside the training environment. Consequences: all grid personas except `normal@0.60` are outside the training distribution; the claim concerns **these checkpoints**; a method-level PPO statement would require X3-B.

## 3. Design
**Personas (generator G1).** Full factorial of the five `SimulatedCandidate` persona types x skills {0.20, 0.30, ..., 0.90} = **40 grid personas**; target difficulty = round(10*skill)/2 (authored rule, not tuned per persona; targets 1.0-4.5 in steps of 0.5). Generated by `x3a_lib.persona_grid()`; the table is written to `personas.csv` and hashed via the library. The five frozen personas (skills 0.20/0.30/0.60/0.80/0.88) form a separate **frozen stratum**, never part of the primary estimate. The training-matched persona is `normal@0.60`.
**Seeds.** 20 evaluation seeds 1001, 2002, ..., 20020 (the first five equal the frozen evaluation seeds); training seeds 42/123/456/789/999; bootstrap RNG seed 42, B = 10 000.
**Conditions (base policy x shield {off, G}).** Constant-Same (= the frozen Fixed policy), Random, Heuristic (frozen definition), Oracle-rule (`oracle_action_from_obs`, R1-R7), Proportional controller (documented as exploiting the persona-target rule; an upper *reference*), PPO (5 checkpoints); PPO state-zero and state-shuffle controls (shield off/on); rule ablations (each of G4, G1, G2, G5, G6 removed) on PPO+G and Constant-Same+G. Exact definitions: config `conditions.definitions`. A behaviour-cloning control is **not** part of X3-A (it needs training; X3-B only). Same seed sets across all conditions.
**Harness validity (Gate G-HARNESS, runs before any result).** The new session loop must reproduce the frozen `run_session_trajectory` exactly on the frozen personas x frozen evaluation seeds for Fixed, Heuristic, PPO raw (5 seeds), PPO+G (5 seeds) and Constant-Same+G (all 9 compared fields identical); the shield copy must equal the frozen shield on 200 000 random/edge states; three mutants (G5 removed; G4 threshold 0.31; heuristic Easier-threshold 0.50) must be **detected**. If any gating check fails the run aborts before producing results. Dry-run history: `research/confirmatory/X3-A/DRYRUN_LOG.md` (two earlier attempts failed because the chosen heuristic mutants were behaviourally invisible on the fixture; recorded, criteria not relaxed).

## 4. Endpoints and inference
**Primary.** Delta = mean over the 40 grid personas and 5 training seeds of [MAE_PPO+G(persona, seed) - MAE_ConstSame+G(persona)], where MAE(persona,.) is the mean session MAE over the 20 evaluation seeds. **Unit: persona**; training seed = second random factor; evaluation seeds are nested and averaged; sessions and turns are descriptive only. **Inference:** two-way cluster bootstrap (resample 40 personas and 5 training seeds with replacement; statistic = mean of the resampled persona x seed cells), B = 10 000, `numpy.random.default_rng(42)`, 95 % percentile interval; effect sizes: mean paired difference (difficulty units) and Cohen's d_z on persona-level differences. With 5 training seeds the seed dimension gives a coarse but reported contribution.
**Secondary (descriptive; same bootstrap; no classification).** volatility, oscillation rate, oracle agreement (fraction of turns on which the raw action equals the oracle action), rule activations, action overrides, attempted boundary actions - for the primary contrast; the contrasts listed in config `secondary_contrasts` (MAE and volatility); rule-ablation effects; strata (training-matched persona, others, persona type, skill band, frozen stratum). Because tracking MAE cannot distinguish oscillation about half-integer targets (registry `P3-C023`), the primary result must be read together with the volatility and oscillation endpoints.

## 5. Sample size rationale
40 personas x 20 evaluation seeds x 5 training seeds. From the frozen-persona accounting (X3-0c) the persona-level SD of the paired difference was 0.0097 (n = 5) and 0.017 in the harness dry-run fixture (n = 5); the implied half-width for 40 personas is about 2.02 x 0.017 / sqrt(40) ~ 0.005, far below delta = 0.12. The sample size is fixed; it was not tuned; the SD estimates were used for this rationale only and set no margin.

## 6. X3-B trigger (frozen)
X3-B is run **iff the X3-A classification is not Equivalent** (Inconclusive, PPO-superior or PPO-adverse), i.e. inconclusive under the equivalence framework or a strong result whose method-level interpretation would need confirmation in a training-consistent environment. It is never triggered from an individual seed or persona. If the trigger fires, X3-B additionally requires the user's decision D-N2 (`research/audit/SPRINT_DECISIONS_AND_BLOCKERS.md`: training candidate seeding/persona distribution, shield-in-loop) and a new tagged protocol; otherwise the branch is parked. If the class is Equivalent, no method-level PPO claim is made and X3-B is not run. (Operationalisation of "strong result" as PPO-superior or PPO-adverse is recorded for the user's review; it does not change any computation in X3-A.)

## 7. Stopping, exclusions, ordering
Fixed personas, seeds, conditions and endpoints; no optional stopping; no persona, seed or condition added, removed or reweighted after data exist; no session excluded; a failed or aborted run is reported and not silently rerun (the runner refuses to start if the results directory exists). Order: environment lock verified -> protocol tag/hashes verified -> G-HARNESS -> full run -> analysis exactly as coded (`x3a_analyze.py`, hashed).

## 8. Interpretation and reporting rules
Equivalent: "no detectable PPO contribution beyond the shield in these checkpoints (within +-0.12 MAE) in this simulator". PPO-superior: a statement about these checkpoints only. PPO-adverse: reported. Inconclusive: never "no effect". Heuristic/oracle/controller comparisons are reported whichever way. Paper 3 terminology: "rule activations" vs "action overrides"; "attempted boundary actions" (never "violations"); PPO is a studied component, not the headline; no learner, learning-gain or deployment claim; simulation only.
