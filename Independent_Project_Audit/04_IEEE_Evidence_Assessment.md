## What the IEEE Paper Should Lead With

Based on the evidence strength above, the technically strongest and most defensible narrative available to this project right now is:

1. **A working, deployed, safety-shielded adaptive-difficulty interview system** (systems contribution — strong evidence).
2. **A rigorous, pre-registered confirmatory test showing the learned RL component's measurable contribution over a deterministic safety shield is not established** — present this as the paper's central empirical finding, not as a disappointment to minimize. A well-designed equivalence/null result, correctly reported with its CI, is a legitimate and interesting contribution, particularly because the project did the methodological work (matched baselines, multi-seed training, pre-registration, held-out confirmatory grid) to make the null result trustworthy rather than merely absent.
3. **A composite, non-LLM answer evaluator with disclosed limitations** (weak-to-moderate human correlation, a length-only baseline that performs comparably, an incompletely documented reasoning-model checkpoint) — framed exactly as the project's own `CLAUDE.md` already frames it: "safety-hardened rather than accuracy-optimal."

## What Should Not Appear

- Any instance of "PPO improves difficulty tracking" or equivalent framing.
- Any instance of the superseded correlation figures (0.9152, 0.8358, 0.7400, 0.6975) presented as current.
- "Guardrails eliminate 100% of out-of-bounds transitions" (still present in the frozen `PAPER3_FINAL_FREEZE.md` banner document; must not be carried into a new paper draft).
- Any reference to the fictional multi-agent coordination-layer architecture from the `Details/`/`capstonePapers/`/`ADCOM2027_Track2_Final/` lineage as if it describes the implemented system.
- The "9/9 contained" / "10/10" / "5/5" aggregate pass-string framings, without the project's own documented downgrade to their scoped, honest versions.

## Negative Results — How to Present Without Undermining Valid Contributions

The project's single most defensible research move available right now is to **present the PPO-vs-constant-action equivalence finding as a deliberate, well-designed result**, not as an admission of failure. The methodological apparatus behind it (multi-seed training, matched conditions, bootstrap CIs, a held-out confirmatory grid, pre-registration) is exactly what makes a null result credible rather than merely inconclusive — this should be stated explicitly in the paper's methodology section as a strength, since it demonstrates the team understood how to test, rather than merely assert, the value of a learned component. This framing is also the only one consistent with the project's own canonical-truth document, which already uses this language.
