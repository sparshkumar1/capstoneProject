# 03 — Research Claims and Evidence: Independent Verification Ledger

**Audit date:** 2026-11-13
**Scope:** Independent, read-only verification of PrepAIred's own scientific-truth and claims-tracking apparatus (`research/CANONICAL_SCIENTIFIC_TRUTH_CURRENT.md`, `research/claims/CLAIM_REGISTRY.csv`, `research/audit/*`), plus a search for superseded numbers appearing loose in non-audit documentation (README, `docs/`, `paper/`).
**Method:** Every file listed in the assignment was read in full or in its entirety where feasible (the two older canonical-truth files, the supersession record, rho-provenance forensic, claim audit, X2 protocol draft, preregistration spec, run-manifest spec, Phase-0 decision lock, RL state-definition audit, PAPER3_FINAL_FREEZE hash manifest, PROJECT_STATE.md, CLAUDE_RESEARCH_INDEX.md, the full 117-row CLAIM_REGISTRY.csv). In addition, independent spot-checks were run directly against the filesystem and git history (not merely trusting the audit prose): SHA-256 of four frozen artifacts was recomputed and compared against both `PAPER3_FINAL_FREEZE.md` and `CLAIM_REGISTRY.csv`; two git commit hashes and one git tag were resolved directly; the "hard-coded constraint_violations: 0" claim was verified by reading the actual script lines; and repo-wide greps were run for every number on the project's own "never cite" list to check whether any appear unflagged outside the audit tree.

Every row/finding below is tagged:
- **VERIFIED FACT** — I independently confirmed this against a primary artifact (file hash, git object, source line), not just the audit's own prose.
- **OBSERVATION** — directly read from a file, not independently re-derived (e.g., a stated statistic I did not recompute).
- **INTERPRETATION** — my own reading of what a set of observations implies.
- **RECOMMENDATION** — an action implied by a finding.
- **UNKNOWN** — could not be verified one way or the other with read-only, in-repo access.

## 1. Headline verdict

The project's own internal audit apparatus (Phase 0, dated 2026-09-19, and a later "Phase 1" layer of replay/analysis work) is unusually rigorous for a student capstone: it already does almost exactly what an external auditor would be asked to do — it identifies its own inflated/stale claims, computes corrected numbers, hashes frozen artifacts, and maintains a 117-row claim registry with per-claim evidence tiers. Independent spot-checks in this audit **did not find any case where the project's self-audit was hiding a worse truth than it stated**. Where I could independently check a specific hash, commit, or source line, the self-audit's version held up. The principal residual risks are (a) **documentation lag**: the top-level `README.md` and some `docs/` files still carry the superseded, stronger-looking numbers as headline claims, without the supersession caveats that `CANONICAL_SCIENTIFIC_TRUTH_CURRENT.md` requires; (b) **one unverifiable provenance claim** (the git tag `v1.0-paper3-complete` is cited repeatedly as the Paper 3 freeze point but does not exist in this local repository's tag list, even though the commit it is supposed to point at does exist); and (c) the self-audit's own "sole authority" document (`CANONICAL_SCIENTIFIC_TRUTH_CURRENT.md`) is itself full of internal evidence-quality caveats (`AUDIT-REPLAY`, `AUDIT-DIAGNOSTIC`, `UNVERIFIED`) that a reader skimming only the headline numbers could miss.

### 4.3 `research/claims/CLAIM_REGISTRY.csv` (117 rows, read in full)
This is the most granular and most load-bearing artifact in the whole audit tree. Full structure documented in `research/claims/README.md` (also read): columns include `claim_id`, `status` (`VALID | HISTORICAL | WITHDRAWN | EXPLORATORY | PENDING-REGISTRATION | NEW-FROZEN | DESIGN-ONLY`), `evidence_label`, `artifact_path`/`sha256`, `script_path`/`commit`, `recompute_tier` (T1 hash-check / T2 recompute-from-data / T3 full re-run), and `verify_cmd`. Rows span four ID families:
- **X-C001…C004** (cross-paper): commit/tag identity facts and the `.venv`-pin-violation fact. All four independently spot-checkable; three of three I tested (X-C001, X-C002, X-C003) matched.
- **P1-C001…C010, P1S-C001…C041** (Paper 1): the latency/concurrency/security/fault-injection/Qwen-isolation claims, each downgraded from an earlier clean-pass framing to a scoped, honestly-caveated one. Notably **P1S-C029** explicitly marks the oft-repeated "SQLite single-file write ceiling is about 150-200 ops/sec" limitation text as WITHDRAWN for having **no supporting measurement** (measured throughput in the actual benchmark was 47-52 ops/s) — this is a case of the audit catching an unsupported *qualitative* claim, not just a wrong number.
- **P2-C001…C013, X2A-C001…C009, X2B-C001…C004** (Paper 2): the evaluator-vs-human claims as summarized in §4.1 §C above, plus the sensitivity/exploratory follow-ups. Notable: **X2B-C003** — a trivial length-only baseline (word count) *matches or exceeds* the derived CrossEncoder on this benchmark (Spearman 0.4897 vs 0.4825), and the registry explicitly states "the old benchmark does not show that R... beats a trivial length baseline... any statement that R 'adds evidence beyond surface features' needs X2-C." This is a genuinely unflattering finding the project chose to register rather than suppress.
- **P3-C001…C024, X3A-C001…C007** (Paper 3): as summarized in §4.1 §D, plus the X3-A confirmatory-grid results, the most important of which is **X3A-C001**: on a held-out 40-persona grid, "PPO+Guardrails minus Constant-Same+Guardrails" MAE difference has a 95% CI of **[-0.0818, 0.0021]** — classified "EQUIVALENT... no detectable PPO contribution beyond the shield in these checkpoints in this simulator."
