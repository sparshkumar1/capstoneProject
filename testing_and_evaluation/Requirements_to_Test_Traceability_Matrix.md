## Final summary (Phase 2/3 complete)

**10 of 12 stated objectives: MET** (with corrections noted where the capstone report's description was inaccurate). **1 of 12 (OBJ-8): confirmed unverifiable**, no stored artifact exists for the specific "93.3%/30 sessions" figure. **1 of 12 (OBJ-11): blocked on an environment gap** (Docker daemon not running), not a code defect — the underlying implementation is confirmed present and correctly configured.

Two genuine defects were found during this verification process and fixed (not merely reported): the guardrail coverage gap (OBJ-2) and the `launch.py` Windows crash (OBJ-9). Both are documented with full diagnosis, rationale, and regression verification in `Phase4_Diagnosis_and_Fixes.md`.

The one known-failing test (`test_stage11_5_coding_adaptation.py::test_coding_turn_preserves_speech_confidence_and_does_not_fabricate_metrics`) remains explicitly unresolved per `CLAUDE.md` — reproduced identically in the final baseline, not touched, pending the user's decision on how to interpret it.
