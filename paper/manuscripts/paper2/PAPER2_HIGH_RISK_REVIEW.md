## Overall assessment
The paper can remain an exploratory diagnostic paper on the current evidence, provided (a) the three interval types and the nominal p-value are labelled everywhere, (b) rater terminology stays at "human reference score" (mean of three recorded ratings for 54 answers, adjudicated scores for 10; "three-rater" only for the 54) with the undocumented items disclosed, (c) the composite-below-components and length findings are stated as findings, and (d) the confirmatory agenda is presented as a requirement list and not as work done.

**Not resolvable without new data or records:** rows 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11, 12, 13 and 16 cannot be closed by any edit to the manuscript. Row 7 (ethics) is the most likely source of a venue-level rejection and is an institutional matter.
