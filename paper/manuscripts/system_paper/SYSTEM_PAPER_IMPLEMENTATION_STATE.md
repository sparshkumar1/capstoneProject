## What the table says
- The live system is **audio + text**. The audio channel reaches adaptation only as one confidence number, used (as itself and as its complement) in PPO and in guardrails; the PPO checkpoint that was analysed barely reacts to it.
- The deployed policy has **no evaluation of its own**: neither P3 nor any human study covers it.
- Only one of the three candidate study conditions (PPO+guardrail) exists in the live system, and it is the unevaluated deployed checkpoint.
