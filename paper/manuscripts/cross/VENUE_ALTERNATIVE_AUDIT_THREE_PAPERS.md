# Venue alternative audit — three papers (evidence date 2026-09-21)

**Scope:** research only. No paper, code, experiment or git state was changed; nothing was submitted. This is the only file written. It extends `cross/FINAL_PUBLICATION_MAP.md` and `cross/VENUE_FIT_MATRIX_V2.md` (which found no fees for any venue).
**No prestige ranking, no acceptance probability, no publication guarantee.**

## 0. Read this first — limits of the evidence
- **Constraints applied:** Indian authors including B.Tech undergraduates; cost ideally ≤ ₹10,000 per accepted paper (preferably ≤ ₹8,000); little international travel; legitimate IEEE Xplore / Springer / ACM routes; genuine scientific fit; deadline open on 21 Sep 2026.
- **Sources:** official conference pages where they could be read. Discovery used web search; several official sites could not be fetched (HTTP 403, certificate error, connection refused: ICDCIT, T4E, IEEE ICALT home, ICAIET home, IEEE CONECCT sub-pages). Where a fact comes from a search result that summarised an official page but the page itself could not be fetched, it is marked **[via search, page not fetched]**.
- **Fees:** almost no 2027 edition has published its fees. Where I give a figure it is labelled with the edition it belongs to. **A previous-edition fee is evidence of scale, not a promise.** Everything else is "not published".
- **Undergraduate authors:** **no venue page states whether undergraduate authors are allowed or excluded.** Only two pages say something related: HCII's *student registration rate* covers "Undergraduate, Masters, or PhD" students; ICETC's student rate applies only if the *first author* is a student (proof required). ISEC and COMPUTE (previous editions) require at least one author to register at a non-student/paper-author rate, so a UG-only paper still needs a faculty co-author's registration; the cost figures below use that rule. The organisers must be asked; nothing here is a guarantee that UG authorship is accepted.
- **Indexing:** IEEE Xplore, Springer and Scopus statements are the conferences' own claims. Scopus was not checked independently. Any wording like "will be submitted / possible inclusion" is flagged; **none of these is a guarantee.**
- **Cost arithmetic:** I do not convert USD to INR (rate not verified); any USD amount below is several times the cap at any plausible rate.
